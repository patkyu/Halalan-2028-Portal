Paano magparehistro

  1. HAKBANG 1

    Pumunta sa OEO

    Go to the Office of the Election Officer for your area, or a satellite or Register Anywhere site in provincial capitals, towns and big cities.

  2. HAKBANG 2

    Fill out CEF-1

    The application form for registration, transfer or reactivation. You can get it at the office.

  3. HAKBANG 3

    Show a valid ID

    Bring at least one valid ID with a photocopy, plus any supporting documents.

  4. HAKBANG 4

    Have your biometrics taken

    Office hours: Tue–Fri 8 a.m.–6 p.m.; Saturdays and holidays 8 a.m.–5 p.m.

Special registration sites (SRAP)

Latest surveys

Bars show each name's share. The black whisker is the margin of error: two names whose whiskers overlap are statistically tied. Tap a name for their profile.

Surveys measure preference among a list the pollster chose, at one point in time. They are not forecasts, and people on the list may not run.

Mahahalagang petsa

    Subukan ang balota

    A practice ballot showing what you will vote for in 2028. Shade ovals to try it. Names are placeholders: the real list comes after COC filing. If you shade more ovals than allowed, none of your votes for that position count.

    Mga puwestong pagbobotohan

    • President and Vice President: six-year terms, elected separately, so voters can split their ticket. President Marcos cannot run again.
    • 12 Senators: the seats won in 2022; winners serve until 2034. Each voter picks up to 12.
    • House of Representatives: district and party-list seats.
    • Local: governors, vice governors, provincial board, mayors, vice mayors, councilors.

    Mga dapat bantayan

    • VP Sara Duterte's impeachment trial is ongoing in the Senate. Senators have said a verdict could come in December 2026, but no date is fixed. A conviction would remove her from office and bar her from holding public office; an acquittal leaves her bid unaffected.
    • Barangay and SK elections were moved from November 2026 to November 2028 under Republic Act 12326.
    • COMELEC budget: COMELEC said it needs about 124,000 counting machines for 2028 and warned that budget cuts could affect preparations.
